Writing Books

I am a retired software architect and engineer with more than four decades of experience building and leading software systems, most recently as Lead Principal Engineer at Atlassian. I am the author of two O'Reilly books and am currently building a production financial planning system as a research instrument for studying correctness in LLM-assisted software development.

My current work focuses on how software engineering must change when implementation tools are probabilistic. The core claim: LLMs don't break software correctness — they relocate where it must be governed.
